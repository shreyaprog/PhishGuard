Security Awareness Guide

  • Phishing relies on trust and urgency, not just technology.
  • HTTPS alone does not prove a site is legitimate or safe.
  • Lookalike domains often imitate a trusted brand with a slightly altered spelling or a deceptive subdomain.
  • Unexpected login links should be verified through official apps or websites, not by the message itself.
  • MFA reduces the impact of credential theft but should be paired with awareness and security controls.
  • If credentials were submitted to a suspicious site, change passwords immediately and review account activity.
